Start with your Friendship email

Check the work email used for your membership.

New Friendship Speaker’s Circle members were processed using their Friendship work email address. Look in that inbox—and your Junk/Spam folder—for your Toastmasters welcome or account-access message. If you cannot find the message or do not remember the password, use Toastmasters’ Forgot Password option. You do not need to wait for the original email if your account is already active.

Do This in Order

From email to Base Camp.

These steps pair the club’s onboarding process with current Toastmasters instructions.

1

Go to Toastmasters.org and sign in

Use the primary email address associated with your Toastmasters membership. For Friendship members, begin with the Friendship work email used when your membership was processed.

Open Toastmasters Login
2

If needed, reset your password

Select Forgot Password, enter your primary email, request the verification code, enter the six-digit code sent by Toastmasters, verify it, and create a new password. Toastmasters says the verification code is valid for 10 minutes.

Reset / Sign InOfficial Login Help
3

Open the Learning Hub and choose a path

After signing in, use Learning Hub → Choose a Path to begin. Toastmasters also provides a Pathways assessment that can recommend three paths based on your skills, interests, and goals. Your first path is included with membership.

Explore PathwaysUse Our Find Your Path Tool
4

Enter Base Camp

Once your path is selected, access your learning materials from the Go to Base Camp card on My Home or in the Learning Hub. This is where your Pathways projects, resources, evaluation materials, and learning experience live.

Begin the Ice Breaker

The Ice Breaker is your first Pathways project. Its purpose is to introduce yourself to the club while practicing the basic structure of a public speech. The current evaluation resource lists a 4–6 minute speech length.

Your First 30 Days

Ice Breaker in one month.

This is the Friendship Speaker’s Circle onboarding target—not a race. If you need more time, communicate with the VPE or your mentor.

DAYS 1–3

Access

  • Find your Toastmasters email.
  • Sign in or reset your password.
  • Watch the recommended Pathways video.
  • Open the Learning Hub.
WEEK 1

Choose

  • Review the six paths.
  • Take the Pathways assessment if helpful.
  • Select your first path.
  • Enter Base Camp and open Level 1.
WEEK 2

Write + Reserve

  • Open the Ice Breaker project.
  • Read the project and evaluation resource.
  • Outline and draft your 4–6 minute speech.
  • Request a prepared-speaking spot by the end of Week 2.
WEEKS 3–4

Practice + Speak

  • Practice aloud and time yourself.
  • Share your evaluation resource with your evaluator.
  • Deliver your Ice Breaker.
  • Review feedback and continue your next project.

Secure your speaking spot.

Prepared Speaker slots are managed in our secure member portal so the VPE can protect meeting timing and Pathways alignment.

1
Choose a Meeting

2
Request Speech

3
Enter Details

Add title, Pathways path, Level/Project, expected length, and presentation needs.

4
VPE Review

Common Questions

What if I get stuck?

I cannot find the Toastmasters email.

Check the Friendship work email used for your membership, including Junk/Spam. If your account was already created, you can go directly to the Toastmasters login page and use Forgot Password with your primary email rather than waiting for the original message.

I forgot my password.
  1. Open Toastmasters Login.
  2. Select Forgot Password.
  3. Enter your primary email.
  4. Select Send Verification Code.
  5. Enter the six-digit code from your email within 10 minutes.
  6. Select Verify Code, continue, and create a new password.
I signed in. Where is Pathways?

Use the Learning Hub. New members can choose a path there. After selection, use the Go to Base Camp card from My Home or the Learning Hub to access learning materials.

Do I have to pay for my first path?

Toastmasters states that every member’s first path is free. Additional paths may have a cost.

What if I have not selected my path yet?

You can still review and begin the Ice Breaker through Toastmasters’ Ice Breaker page while path access or selection is being completed.

How do I get on the Friendship Speaker’s Circle agenda?

Use the secure Member Portal, select the meeting, open Request Speech, enter your speech details, and submit. The VPE reviews requests and available Prepared Speaker slots.
