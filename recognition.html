Signature Recognition

Six ways we celebrate members.

Each category recognizes a different kind of contribution so recognition does not become a popularity contest or reward only the most experienced speakers.

🌟

Member of the Month

Recognizes a member whose participation, encouragement, preparation, and positive contribution strengthen the club community.

Looks for: Consistency • participation • encouragement Rhythm: Monthly
🎙️

Speech Spotlight

Celebrates a prepared speech that connects with the audience, demonstrates strong preparation, or shows meaningful improvement.

Looks for: Connection • preparation • growth Rhythm: Meeting / monthly spotlight
🏅

Pathways Achievement

Recognizes members who complete educational projects, levels, or other meaningful Pathways milestones.

Looks for: Progress • persistence • completion Rhythm: As earned
🤝

Service Recognition

Honors members who step up for roles, mentor others, solve problems, support events, or quietly help the club succeed.

Looks for: Service • reliability • leadership Rhythm: As earned
🔔

Golden Buzzer

A special annual recognition for a standout moment that captures courage, connection, excellence, growth, or the spirit of the club.

Looks for: Memorable impact • club spirit Rhythm: Once per program year
🚀

Growth Award

Highlights visible improvement over time. This award celebrates movement, effort, and application of feedback—not simply the strongest starting performance.

Looks for: Improvement • feedback • courage Rhythm: Quarterly / year-end
How Recognition Works

Simple, fair, and visible.

The goal is a repeatable system that the club can sustain from year to year.

1

Notice

Members and leaders pay attention to growth, service, participation, speeches, milestones, and standout moments.

2

Review

Use meeting results, participation records, Pathways progress, and the Awards Tracker to ground recognition in real contributions.

3

Celebrate

Recognize the member publicly during a meeting or club communication and explain what specifically earned the recognition.

4

Record

Log the recognition in the Awards Tracker so the club maintains a history of milestones and accomplishments.

Recognition Criteria

What we want to reinforce.

A member does not need to be perfect to be recognized. These are the behaviors that move the club forward.

🔥Courage
📈Growth
🤝Service
✅Consistency
👑Leadership
Recognition principle: Tell members why they are being recognized. Specific recognition teaches the entire club what excellence, growth, service, and leadership look like.
Once Per Program Year

The Golden Buzzer

This is the club’s rarest recognition—a standout moment that makes the room feel different. It may come from a speech, an act of leadership, a breakthrough, extraordinary service, or a moment that captures the spirit of Friendship Speaker’s Circle.

The point is not perfection. The point is impact.
