Member Contribution Map

You do not have to be an officer to move the plan.

🎤 Speak

Request prepared speeches, complete Pathways projects, and use feedback to keep moving toward education awards.

✅ Serve

Take meeting roles, prepare before meetings, and help every meeting feel purposeful and worth attending.

👋 Invite

Bring a colleague, welcome guests, and help prospective members understand what the club can offer.

🤝 Connect

Use your mentor, check on another member, and help remove barriers that keep people from participating.

🏆 Recognize

Celebrate courage, growth, service, consistency, Pathways milestones, and leadership so the culture is reinforced.

📣 Share

Tell the club’s story, promote events, and help Friendship colleagues see communication and leadership growth in action.
