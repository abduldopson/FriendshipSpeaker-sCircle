Calendar Standard

Dates are planned, not locked forever.

When a meeting must move, the club should update the live calendar, role sign-up workbook, and meeting announcement together. That keeps members from receiving conflicting information.

Default location: Announced with each meeting invitation until a specific meeting location is confirmed.

Importable Calendar File

The calendar is packaged as an .ics file, which can be imported into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and most calendar apps.
