- **10 campus/network communities** represented.
- **5 broad professional role families** represented.

## Open House feedback
- 16/16 rated the club's welcome **4 or 5 out of 5** (100%).
- 15/16 rated increased understanding of Toastmasters **4 or 5 out of 5** (93.8%).
- 13/16 rated respondents said they were **Very Interested** in joining.

## Public-site privacy approach
The website displays only aggregate patterns. It does not publish names, email addresses, phone numbers, or individual survey responses.

## Methodology notes
- Duplicate interest survey submissions were removed using normalized name/email matching.
- Campus labels were normalized for consistent reporting.
- Role titles were grouped into broad public-facing categories.
- Skill-improvement questions allowed multiple selections, so percentages do not sum to 100%.
