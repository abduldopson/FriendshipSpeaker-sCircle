How It Works

From club stage to larger stages.

Official contests originate at club level and progress through Area, Division, and District. The International Speech Contest continues beyond District through quarterfinals and semifinals toward the World Championship of Public Speaking®.

Official Contest Types

Six ways to challenge your speaking.

5–7 minutes

International Speech

A prepared speech on a subject of the contestant’s choice. This is the contest every District must conduct.

2–3 minute evaluation

Evaluation

Contestants observe a 5–7 minute test speech and then deliver an evaluation of that speech.

Prepared humorous speech

Humorous

A thematic humorous speech with an opening, body, and close—not simply a series of unrelated one-liners.

Impromptu

Table Topics®

Every contestant receives the same topic and learns it only when introduced by the contest chair.

Tall tale

Tall Tales

A speech built around a highly exaggerated and improbable theme or plot.

5–7 minutes

Online Speech

A prepared speech presented live online by contestants participating from physically distant locations.

Contest Readiness

Start preparing before a contest is announced.

As a contestant

Practice consistently, know the official rules, time your speech, seek targeted feedback, rehearse under contest conditions, and verify eligibility before entering.

Request a Practice SpeechBuild Your Speaking Skills

As a club member

Help create contest-ready speakers by giving strong evaluations, volunteering for contest roles, attending practice sessions, and encouraging members to test new material.
