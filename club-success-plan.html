Know the plan. Help move it.

The Club Success Plan turns the Distinguished Club Program into a yearlong operating plan for member growth, club quality, leadership, and measurable follow-through.

Our North Star

Four areas of focus.

The plan keeps member growth at the center of club decisions.

🤝

Member Experience

Prepared meetings, supportive feedback, mentoring, and recognition.

🎓

Education

Steady Pathways progress and visible milestones.

🌱

Membership

Recruit, onboard, engage, and retain members.

🧭

Leadership & Operations

Trained officers, on-time administration, and clear action ownership.

Primary recognition target: President’s Distinguished — submit the Club Success Plan by September 30, finish with at least 20 members, and achieve at least 9 of 10 DCP goals. Stretch target: Smedley Distinguished — finish with at least 25 members and achieve all 10 goals.
September Focus

Urgency means knowing what matters now.

The plan calls for submission by September 30 and a clear Pathways completion pipeline. It also prioritizes confirming the membership base, officer training, board mentors, and a monthly DCP review rhythm.

Member Contribution Map

You do not have to be an officer to move the plan.

🎤 Speak

Request prepared speeches, complete Pathways projects, and use feedback to keep moving toward education awards.

✅ Serve

Take meeting roles, prepare before meetings, and help every meeting feel purposeful and worth attending.

👋 Invite

Bring a colleague, welcome guests, and help prospective members understand what the club can offer.

🤝 Connect

Use your mentor, check on another member, and help remove barriers that keep people from participating.

🏆 Recognize

Celebrate courage, growth, service, consistency, Pathways milestones, and leadership so the culture is reinforced.

📣 Share

Tell the club’s story, promote events, and help Friendship colleagues see communication and leadership growth in action.
