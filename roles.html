Role Training Center

Meeting Roles

Every role is a leadership opportunity. Use these guides so you know what to prepare, what to say, and how to hand control back professionally.

Prepare → Perform → Handoff

Know your job before you get the floor.

Role explanations should be brief. Your preparation happens before the meeting—not while everyone is waiting.

🎙️

Toastmaster of the Day

The meeting’s primary host and connector.

Before the meeting

Confirm the agenda, theme, speakers, and role holders.

During the meeting

Lead transitions, introduce major segments, protect the flow, and keep energy up.

After the role

Thank participants and note anything to improve next time.

⚡

Table Topics Master

Leads the impromptu speaking portion.

Before the meeting

Prepare inclusive, open-ended questions tied to the theme when appropriate.

During the meeting

Explain Table Topics, ask the question before naming the participant, manage time, and encourage participation.

After the role

Return control clearly and share any notes needed for recognition.

🔎

General Evaluator

Evaluates the meeting as a whole.

Before the meeting

Confirm the evaluation team and understand each role.

During the meeting

Introduce the team, lead the evaluation segment, and comment on organization, transitions, timing, and effectiveness.

After the role

Share one or two meeting-level improvements.

📝

Speech Evaluator

Provides supportive, specific feedback to a prepared speaker.

Before the meeting

Review the speaker’s project objective and ask what feedback would be most useful.

During the meeting

Identify strengths, cite evidence, and give actionable recommendations.

After the role

Encourage the speaker’s next step.

⏱️

Timer

Protects speaking time and reports actual times.

Before the meeting

Know every time requirement and timing signal.

During the meeting

Display timing signals consistently and record times accurately.

After the role

Report times clearly and briefly.

📚

Grammarian

Listens for language use and introduces the Word of the Day.

Before the meeting

Choose a useful Word of the Day and prepare its definition and example.

During the meeting

Track memorable language, effective phrasing, and Word of the Day usage.

After the role

Give a concise language report.

👂

Ah-Counter

Tracks filler words and distracting verbal habits.

Before the meeting

Know the filler words and sounds to listen for.

During the meeting

Record repeated fillers without interrupting speakers.

After the role

Report patterns respectfully and without embarrassment.

🔔

Sergeant at Arms

Opens and closes the meeting and supports meeting readiness.

Before the meeting

Confirm room or virtual setup, materials, and basic logistics.

During the meeting

Call the meeting to order, review etiquette, and make the first formal handoff.

After the role

Adjourn formally and support cleanup or room reset.
