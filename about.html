About Our Club

We create space for voices to grow.

Our Mission

Practice. Feedback. Mentoring. Service.

We provide a supportive and positive learning experience in which members develop communication and leadership skills through practice, feedback, mentoring, and service.

That mission shows up in the way we structure meetings, prepare members for roles, support new speakers, celebrate progress, and create leadership opportunities throughout the club.

Our goal is not simply to host meetings. Our goal is to help people leave each meeting more confident, more prepared, and more willing to use their voice.

Our Vision

A club where people feel safe enough to practice and challenged enough to grow.

We want Friendship Speaker’s Circle to be known as a place where communication and leadership development are practical, supportive, consistent, and connected to the real work our members do every day.

Together, We Rise. Together, We Thrive.

Toastmasters Core Values

The values behind how we operate.

Integrity, Respect, Service, and Excellence shape the way we lead meetings, give feedback, support members, and represent the club.

01

Integrity

We communicate honestly, follow through on commitments, and act with trustworthiness in club responsibilities.

02

Respect

We value each member’s voice, experiences, skills, abilities, and contributions while creating a supportive learning environment.

03

Service

We strengthen the club by being responsive, prepared, helpful, and willing to contribute to the success of others.

04

Excellence

We work to improve our meetings, speeches, leadership, feedback, systems, and member experience over time.

The Member Experience

What makes this club work.

Members are not expected to arrive as polished speakers. The system is designed to help people practice intentionally and grow over time.

🎤

Real Practice

Members learn by doing: prepared speeches, Table Topics, evaluations, meeting roles, facilitation, and leadership assignments.

📝

Useful Feedback

Feedback is specific, respectful, and focused on helping members understand what worked and what to practice next.

🤝

Mentoring

Members receive support from club leadership and mentors who help them navigate roles, speeches, Pathways, and participation.

🧭

Clear Systems

Members have access to meeting guides, role resources, calendars, trackers, forms, and a Member Hub built to reduce confusion.

🏆

Recognition

We celebrate courage, improvement, service, consistency, milestones, and the contributions that strengthen the club community.

👑

Leadership

Members grow beyond speeches by taking ownership, mentoring others, leading meetings, and serving the club.

Our Promise to Members

You should know what to expect when you walk into the room.

Friendship Speaker’s Circle is designed to be supportive without being passive. We encourage people, but we also create structure, accountability, and repeated opportunities to practice.

A respectful environment for learning and practice.
Clear expectations for roles and meetings.
Feedback intended to build, not embarrass.
Opportunities to speak, lead, mentor, and serve.
Recognition for meaningful growth and contribution.
Connected to Friendship

Professional growth that supports the larger Friendship community.

Friendship Speaker’s Circle serves as a professional communication and leadership community connected to Friendship Public Charter School. The club gives members a place to strengthen skills that transfer directly into meetings, classrooms, presentations, collaboration, coaching, family engagement, and leadership.

Friendship Homepage

The club also connects members to Friendship’s broader professional learning ecosystem through resources such as the Friendship Talent Hub while maintaining Toastmasters as the source for official Toastmasters program requirements.
