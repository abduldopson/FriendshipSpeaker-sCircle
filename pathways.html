Speech Preparation

A simple planning process.

1

Know the Objective

Read the official project requirements and understand what skill you are practicing.

2

Choose One Message

Decide what you want the audience to think, feel, or do after your speech.

3

Build the Structure

Opening → key points/story → takeaway → closing.

4

Practice Out Loud

Time yourself and rehearse delivery—not just the words on the page.

5

Tell Your Evaluator What You Need

Ask for targeted feedback that supports your current growth goal.

6

Reflect & Revise

Record one strength and one next-step goal after every prepared speech.

Interactive Member Tool

Find Your Path

Answer 10 quick questions about the skills you most want to build. You’ll receive a primary Pathways recommendation and a second path worth exploring.

1. What growth would make the biggest difference for you right now?
2. Which speaking challenge sounds most interesting?
3. In leadership, you most want to practice…
4. What kind of audience response do you most want?
5. Which workplace skill would you most like to strengthen?
6. Which project sounds most energizing?
7. When feedback gets uncomfortable, what skill do you want?
8. What would you most like people to say about your communication?
9. Which kind of growth feels most like your next chapter?
10. Pick the statement that sounds most like your goal.
🧭

What this measures

Your answers are compared across the six currently offered core Pathways choices: Dynamic Leadership, Engaging Humor, Motivational Strategies, Persuasive Influence, Presentation Mastery, and Visionary Communication.
