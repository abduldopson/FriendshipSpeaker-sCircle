Board Rhythm

A simple operating cadence.

The Officer Hub should reduce last-minute scrambling by giving the board a predictable cycle for planning and follow-through.

Before Each Board Meeting

  • Review action items from the previous meeting.
  • Add updates to the agenda before the meeting.
  • Check member needs, Pathways progress, and upcoming roles.
  • Bring only issues that require discussion, decision, or support.

During the Board Meeting

  • Keep discussion tied to decisions and outcomes.
  • Assign an owner and due date to every action item.
  • Capture decisions in the minutes.
  • Protect time for member experience and club growth.

After the Board Meeting

  • Update the action tracker.
  • Send follow-up notes and deadlines.
  • Contact assigned members or mentors.
  • Close completed tasks before the next board meeting.

Officer Checklist

What healthy club leadership looks like.

☐ Members know what is happening and what is expected.
☐ Upcoming roles are filled and members are prepared.
☐ New members are onboarded and connected to a mentor.
☐ Pathways progress is visible and celebrated.
☐ Officer commitments have owners and due dates.
☐ Training, dues, and administrative deadlines are current.
☐ Recognition is intentional, consistent, and documented.
Mentoring Responsibility

Board members also develop people.

Officer leadership is not limited to meetings and paperwork. Board members help members stay connected, prepare for roles, navigate Pathways, and grow into greater responsibility.

Use mentor assignments and regular check-ins to make sure no member disappears simply because nobody followed up.

Board Accountability

Every discussion should end with a next step.

The Officer Hub is designed around one simple expectation: if the board identifies a need, concern, opportunity, or commitment, it should leave the meeting with an owner and a due date.
